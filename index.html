Our Vision & Mission

Vision

A welcoming Shul of dedication and warmth, forged from the combined strengths of two communities.

Mission

We offer a warm and meaningful place for Shabbos davening. Hopefully, IY”H, as our community grows, we look forward to additional daily minyanim and regular shiurim, at the Shul; thus expanding its scope and mission, to be evermore beneficial to our community.

Monthly Community Meal

Once a month, our whole community gathers for a meal together — open to everyone, no matter how new you are or how long you’ve been here.

A donation is warmly appreciated — but never required. Come as you are; the meal is about being together, not about what you give.

To RSVP: ✉️ info@beisahavasyisroel.org  ·  📞 (347) 528-0755
