Our Vision & Mission

Vision

A welcoming shtibel of seriousness and warmth — built from the strengths of two communities.

Mission

We offer a warm and serious place for Shabbos davening. As our community grows, we hope to add daily minyanim and regular shiurim, remaining a stable and welcoming shtibel sustained by its ba’alei batim.

Monthly Community Meal

Once a month, our whole community gathers for a meal together — open to everyone, no matter how new you are or how long you’ve been here.

A donation is warmly appreciated — but never required. Come as you are; the meal is about being together, not about what you give.
